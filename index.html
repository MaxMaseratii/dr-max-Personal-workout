How to eat today

Food and water for the work, not a diet brand. Adjust portions to your body weight — protein is the number that matters most.

Water
3.0–3.5 L
Protein
1.8 g / kg
Meals
4 + snack

Example: 80 kg person → about 145 g protein a day. If you want to lean out, keep carbs a bit lower at dinner. If you want to grow, add a cup of rice or oats.

Water today

3.2 L = 8 glasses of 400 ml. Drink one glass when you wake, one after training, then sip through the day. Finish 1 hour before lights out so sleep stays clean.

Everyday meal plan

Eat this pattern 6 training days. On Sunday rest, keep the same meals but you can drop the post-workout shake if you prefer whole food.

4:50 AM · pre-workout (optional)
Banana + black coffee, or 200 ml water if you train fasted
Keep it light so the 5:15 session does not sit heavy. If you feel flat, add a spoon of honey.
6:40 AM · after training
Protein shake + 1 fruit, or 3 eggs + toast
25–40 g protein in the first hour after lifting. This is the meal that feeds the back and shoulders you just trained.
12:30 PM · lunch
Palm-size chicken, fish, lean beef or tofu · 1 fist rice / potato · big salad or veg
Add olive oil or avocado. This should be your biggest meal on training days.
4:00 PM · snack
Greek yoghurt + berries, or cottage cheese, or a second shake
20 g protein. Stops the 5 PM crash that usually turns into junk food.
7:00–7:30 PM · dinner
Protein + vegetables + a smaller carb (or extra veg if leaning out)
Finish eating by 8:00 PM so 9:00 lights-out is realistic. Keep it low-oil and low-sugar.

Simple plate rules

Every plate: protein first, then vegetables, then carbs around training.

Drink: water, black coffee, tea. Skip juice and fizzy drinks — they hide a lot of sugar.

Salt & minerals: a pinch of salt on meals plus a banana or yoghurt covers most early-morning sessions.

Off-limits most days: late-night takeaway, alcohol on training nights, “I earned this” desserts after every workout.

When you will see a change

If you hit the workouts, protein, water and 9 PM sleep. Miss those and the calendar stretches.

7 days
Better energy at 5 AM, less bloating, sleep feels deeper. The mirror barely moves yet — that is normal.
14–21 days
Stronger pumps, clothes at the waist feel a bit easier, pull-ups and presses go up. Friends may not notice. You will.
6–8 weeks
Shoulders look wider, waist looks tighter, back has more lines in photos. This is the first real “V” you can see.
12–16 weeks
Clear V-taper in a T-shirt if you stayed consistent. That is one full training block — not a weekend reset.
